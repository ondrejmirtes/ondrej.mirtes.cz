Plenty of services make money off users who don't actively use them but haven't canceled their subscription. It's one of the many well-known [dark patterns](http://darkpatterns.org/what_is_a_dark_pattern/).

The founders of the Beeminder startup are [convinced](http://blog.beeminder.com/autocancel/) that an honest approach is more advantageous for both sides—one where the user only pays for the service at the moment they're actually using it.
