Jeff Atwood (aka [Coding Horror](https://web.archive.org/web/20130208131741/http://www.codinghorror.com:80/blog/)), co-founder of Stack Overflow, has set out on his "next big thing".

He's [bothered](https://web.archive.org/web/20130211055038/http://www.codinghorror.com:80/blog/2013/02/civilized-discourse-construction-kit.html?) by the state of discussion forums on the Internet, which have barely changed since their inception. With his Discourse platform he's trying to stir up these stagnant waters with a host of innovative ideas.

The project is [open-source](https://github.com/discourse/discourse) and written in Rails.
