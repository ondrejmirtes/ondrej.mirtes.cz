A new project by [Marco Arment](http://www.marco.org), the author of [Instapaper](http://www.instapaper.com/), for iOS devices with a very simple and traditional business model — for two dollars a month you get access to all past and future issues. Each one contains 4–6 articles from personalities of the global blogosphere, and so far it has always been very readable material.

There's also a weekly trial version, which likewise gives you access to everything I mentioned here.
