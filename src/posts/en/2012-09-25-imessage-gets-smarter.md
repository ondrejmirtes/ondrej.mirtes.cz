iOS 6 and OS X 10.8.2 fix the iMessage problems I [mentioned](/en/confusion-around-imessage) three weeks ago. Once you install these updates, messages sent to your phone number will start arriving on your iPad and Mac as well.

As a nice bonus, all of your devices will stop ringing constantly when you’re in the middle of a conversation on just one of them.
