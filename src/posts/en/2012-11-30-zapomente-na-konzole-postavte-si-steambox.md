The Verge came up with an idea for how to get around the long life cycle of today's gaming consoles and treat yourself to high-end graphics in the living room right now. They used the [Steam Big Picture](http://store.steampowered.com/bigpicture/) feature, which is made precisely for this purpose. And most games on Windows reportedly support the Xbox 360 controller.

I absolutely love this idea, especially because on the Mac I can only play games from Valve and Blizzard, and most of my Steam collection sits idle.
