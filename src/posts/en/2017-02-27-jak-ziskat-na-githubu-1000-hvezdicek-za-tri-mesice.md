At the beginning of December 2016 I released PHPStan, and it's been enjoying great popularity ever since. It currently has over 1,300 stars [on GitHub](https://github.com/phpstan/phpstan) and over 30,000 downloads on Packagist. It makes me really happy and I love working on it.

On the [PHPStan blog I wrote at length](https://phpstan.org/blog/how-i-got-from-0-to-1000-stars-on-github-in-three-months-with-my-open-source-side-project) about how to go about it if you too want to create a useful, high-quality and successful open-source project. Spoiler alert: it's not just about the code!
