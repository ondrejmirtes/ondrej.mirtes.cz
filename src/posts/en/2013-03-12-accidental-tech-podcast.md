After wrapping up [their](http://www.marco.org/2012/11/18/build-analyze-ending) [shows](http://5by5.tv/hypercritical/100) in December, Marco Arment and John Siracusa couldn't last long without podcasting. As early as the second half of January they started a new show, [Neutral](http://neutral.fm/), about cars, and since they realized that after the show they'd keep talking about tech for another hour, they turned that into a podcast too.

I have [one episode](http://atp.fm/episodes/3-conditions-led-to-freecell) behind me, and I have to say that listening to these two share the airwaves is a joy in itself.
